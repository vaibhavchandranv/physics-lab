1.7

energy, work & power

energy stores

energy can be stored as kinetic, gravitational potential, chemical, elastic (strain), nuclear, electrostatic, and internal (thermal).

1.7.1 | energy

types of energy stores

  • kinetic energy - energy of moving objects
  • gravitational potential energy - energy due to height in a gravitational field
  • chemical energy - energy stored in chemical bonds
  • elastic (strain) energy - energy stored in deformed elastic materials
  • nuclear energy - energy stored in atomic nuclei
  • electrostatic energy - energy due to electric charges
  • internal (thermal) energy - energy due to particle motion

energy transfer between stores

  • mechanical work - energy transfer by forces causing motion
  • heating - energy transfer due to temperature difference
  • radiation - energy transfer via electromagnetic waves

kinetic energy

$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$ (unit: J). if velocity doubles, kinetic energy quadruples.

gravitational potential energy

$GPE = mgh$ (unit: J), where g = 9.8 m/s².

conservation of energy

energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred between stores.

sankey diagrams

diagrams where arrow width represents energy transfer.

1.7.2 | work

definition

work is done when a force causes displacement in its direction.

calculating work

$$W = Fd$$

  • $W = Fd$ (force parallel to motion)
  • $W = \Delta E$ (work equals energy change)

work

$W = Fd = \Delta E$ (unit: J)

1.7.3 | energy resources

  • fossil fuels - chemical energy, non-renewable
  • biofuels - renewable but land intensive
  • geothermal - heat from earth
  • nuclear fuels - fission energy
  • solar - light to electricity
  • wind - turbine energy
  • water - hydro, tides, waves

efficiency

$\% \, \text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful output}}{\text{total input}} \times 100$

no system is 100% efficient; energy is always lost as heat.

nuclear fusion in the sun

hydrogen nuclei fuse to form helium, releasing energy.

1.7.4 | power

definition

power is the rate of energy transfer.

calculating power

$$P = \frac{W}{t}$$

$$P = \frac{\Delta E}{t}$$

power

$P = \frac{W}{t} = \frac{\Delta E}{t}$ (unit: W)
