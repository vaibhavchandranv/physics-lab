1.6

momentum

definition

$$p = mv$$

(unit: kg·m/s). heavier or faster objects have greater momentum.

impulse and force

$$I = F\Delta t = \Delta (mv)$$

applying a large force briefly gives same impulse as small force over long time. this is why seatbelts increase impact time, reducing force.

conservation of momentum

in collisions and explosions with no external forces, total momentum is conserved. momentum before = momentum after.

$$m_1u_1 + m_2u_2 = m_1v_1 + m_2v_2$$

where u1 and u2 are initial velocities and v1 and v2 are final velocities.

resultant force and momentum

$$F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$$

a larger force produces faster change in momentum.

momentum

$p = mv$

a measure of how difficult it is to stop a moving object.

impulse

$I = F\Delta t = \Delta (mv)$

conservation of momentum

total momentum before = total momentum after.

force from momentum
