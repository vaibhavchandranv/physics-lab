4.1

turning effects of forces

centre of gravity

the centre of gravity (cg) is the point through which the entire weight of an object may be considered to act.

  • for a uniform object, the centre of gravity lies at its geometrical centre
  • an object balances when its centre of gravity lies directly above its support
  • if the line of action of the weight falls outside the base, the object topples

moments

a moment is the turning effect of a force about a pivot.

$$\text{moment}=F\times d$$

  • $F$ = force
  • $d$ = perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force
  • si unit: $\mathrm{Nm}$

the greater the force or perpendicular distance, the greater the turning effect.

perpendicular distance

the distance used when calculating moments is always the perpendicular distance, not simply the length of the object.

$$d=l\cos\theta$$

$$\text{moment}=Fl\cos\theta$$

  • measure the shortest distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force
  • never use the full length unless it is already perpendicular

clockwise and anticlockwise moments

a force may produce either a clockwise or an anticlockwise turning effect, depending on the direction in which it tends to rotate the object.

  • clockwise moments are usually taken as positive or negative depending on convention
  • use the same sign convention throughout a calculation

couples and torque

a couple consists of two forces that are:

  • equal in magnitude
  • opposite in direction
  • parallel
  • acting along different lines of action

a couple produces rotation without causing translation.

the turning effect of a couple is called torque.

$$\tau=F\times d$$

$$\tau=F(2r)$$

  • $d$ is the perpendicular distance between the two forces
  • if each force acts a distance $r$ from the centre, then $d=2r$

summary

  • moments describe the turning effect of a force
  • always use the perpendicular distance
  • larger force or distance produces a larger moment
  • a couple produces pure rotation and no resultant force
  • torque is the turning effect produced by a couple
