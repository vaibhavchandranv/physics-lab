6.2

elastic and plastic behaviour

elastic deformation

elastic deformation is a temporary change in the shape or size of an object.

when the applied force is removed, the object returns to its original shape and size.

plastic deformation

plastic deformation is a permanent change in the shape or size of an object.

when the applied force is removed, the object does not return to its original shape and remains permanently deformed.

elastic limit

the elastic limit is the greatest load or stress that can be applied to a material without causing permanent deformation.

below the elastic limit, the material behaves elastically. beyond the elastic limit, plastic deformation occurs.

force-extension graph

a force-extension graph shows how the extension of a material changes as the applied force increases.

initially, force is directly proportional to extension. this straight-line region obeys hooke's law.

beyond the limit of proportionality, the graph begins to curve and hooke's law no longer applies.

if the elastic limit is exceeded, the material undergoes plastic deformation and does not return to its original length when the force is removed.

elastic potential energy

elastic potential energy is the energy stored in an object when it is elastically deformed.

the energy stored is equal to the work done in stretching or compressing the object.

this is equal to the area under the force-extension graph.

$$E_p=\frac{1}{2}Fx$$

  • \(E_p\) = elastic potential energy (J)
  • \(F\) = applied force (N)
  • \(x\) = extension or compression (m)

if the material obeys hooke's law, where \(F=kx\),

$$E_p=\frac{1}{2}kx^2$$

  • \(k\) = spring constant (N m-1)
  • \(x\) = extension or compression (m)

important notes

  • elastic deformation is reversible, while plastic deformation is permanent.
  • hooke's law is obeyed only up to the limit of proportionality.
  • the elastic limit marks the greatest stress or load that does not produce permanent deformation.
  • the area under a force-extension graph represents the work done on the material.
  • for materials obeying hooke's law, the area under the graph is a triangle, giving \(E_p=\frac12Fx\).
  • elastic potential energy is measured in joules (J).
